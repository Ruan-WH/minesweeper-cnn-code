Figure 7. Distribution of test win rates for different training strategies on the 9 x 9 Minesweeper task with 18 mines. The four checkpoints were selected using online training win rates (Best). Each boxplot summarizes ten evaluation groups of 1,000 games for a single trained checkpoint. Boxes show the interquartile ranges, center lines indicate medians, and whiskers extend to the most extreme observations within 1.5 times the interquartile range. Circles show individual group win rates; diamonds and numerical labels denote the mean win rates. These distributions represent variation across evaluation groups, not across independent training runs. Overall win rates and Wilson 95% confidence intervals are reported in Table 4.
