Section 3.5 methods text (results paragraph remains pending):
We additionally evaluated a constraint-based Minesweeper solver adapted from
nabenabe0928/mine-sweeper-solver (commit
3a499ddee44bf7562808c8ab787bd173fb68fa2e, Apache-2.0). The agent uses only revealed
clues, unknown-cell locations, and the public total mine count. Deterministic
constraint propagation identifies safe cells and mines. When no safe action is
established, the solver enumerates feasible frontier assignments and weights
them by the number of compatible non-frontier mine placements. It selects the
unknown cell with minimum mine probability, breaking ties by row-major index.

Each decision is limited to 20,000 search iterations; no wall-clock cutoff is
used. If enumeration exceeds this budget, incomplete configuration counts are
discarded and an explicitly logged heuristic risk estimate is used. No-frontier
uniform probabilities are recorded separately from heuristic fallbacks. All
games remain in the evaluation denominator, including any recorded errors.
The solver is evaluated on 9 x 9 boards with 10, 14, 18, and 22 mines, with 10,000
games per condition and ten consecutive groups of 1,000 games. Episode seeds
are (20261013 + episode index) modulo 2^32. The first click is the top-left cell,
and the original environment guarantees safety without requiring a zero clue.
